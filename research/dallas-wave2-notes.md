## Verification decisions

- **Kidd 1996 effective date is December 26**, following [Dallas's 2024–25 historical ledger](https://cdn.nba.com/teams/uploads/sites/1610612742/2025/09/2024-25-Dallas-Mavericks-Media-Guide-2_compressed.pdf). It includes a second in **1997 or 1998**, not an unconditional second assigned retrospectively to 1998. The exact original timing trigger is not enumerated, so the obligation retains that uncertainty. [The contemporary 1998 draft list](https://www.deseret.com/1998/6/25/19387811/1998-nba-draft-selections/) verifies Phoenix-origin No. 53 and Greg Buckner. The same Dallas ledger confirms the entire nine-player February 17, 1997 Cassell exchange.
- **Kidd 2001 is July 18**, the completed trade date, rather than the June agreement. [Phoenix's historical media guide](https://library.sfo2.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/publications/basketball/yearbooks/KPHXSMG-2013.pdf) includes Chris Dudley with Kidd and all three players going to Phoenix.
- **Kidd 2008 uses the final eight-player package**, including Antoine Wright, Keith Van Horn's sign-and-trade, two Dallas firsts and $3 million. The abandoned Devean George proposal is excluded. [The completed report](https://www.dallasobserver.com/news/done-deal-kidds-a-mav-again-7137854) and Dallas guide corroborate it. [NetsDaily's contemporaneous ledger](https://www.netsdaily.com/2008/2/24/1346557/post-kmart-vs-post-jkidd) identifies 2008 lottery protection and the unprotected 2010 first. Unused 2008 rollover terms are not invented. [The 2008 draft list](https://www.deseret.com/2008/6/27/20260779/2008-nba-draft-selections/) verifies Dallas-origin No. 21/Ryan Anderson.
- **Carter 2004:** early reporting reversed the Philadelphia/Denver pick years. The [completed Nets announcement](https://www.nba.com/nets/news/carter121704.html) and [contemporaneous completed report](https://www.netsdaily.com/2004/12/17/1347749/nets-acquire-vince-carter-from) establish Philadelphia 2005 and Denver 2006. The [2008 ledger](https://www.netsdaily.com/2008/2/24/1346557/post-kmart-vs-post-jkidd) independently confirms PHI top 8/5/unprotected in 2005/06/07 and DEN top 5/2/unprotected in 2006/07/08. [The 2005 draft record](https://score-origin.espn.com/nba/draft/rounds/_/season/2005) identifies the PHI-origin pick as Joey Graham at No. 16. The Denver pick is deliberately not followed.
- **Carter 2009 and 2010** use the full packages in [Orlando's historical guide](https://library.sfo2.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/publications/basketball/yearbooks/KORLMMG-2013.pdf), [UPI's completed 2009 report](https://www.upi.com/Sports_News/2009/06/25/Nets-trade-Vince-Carter-to-Orlando/69081245979758/) and [Phoenix's 2010 announcement](https://www.nba.com/suns/news/feature_improve_trade_101218.html). Orlando's separate Lewis/Arenas transaction is not combined. The Orlando 2011 first and cash are included; conditions and cash amount absent from the cited release are explicitly not asserted.
- **Barkley 1992** follows [UPI's June 17 report](https://www.upi.com/Archives/1992/06/17/Barkley-traded-to-Suns/3478708753600/) and Phoenix's guide. **Barkley 1996 includes a 1999 second**, easily missed in shorter retrospectives. [The Houston account](https://www.houstonpress.com/news/westbrook-latest-former-franchise-icon-to-become-a-rocket-11323229/) and [contemporary Horry profile](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1997-01-11-sp-17533-story.html) support it. [The contemporary 1999 order](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/1999-nba-draft-order/) independently confirms Phoenix origin. Its downstream use is not published here.
- **Wilt 1965** follows the January 15 date and complete package in [Golden State's ledger](https://ak-static-int.nba.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2016/10/2016-17-Media-Guide-Warriors.pdf), cross-checked with [Philadelphia's guide](https://library.sfo2.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/publications/basketball/yearbooks/KPHI7MG-2020.pdf). Canonical franchise `GSW` is labeled San Francisco Warriors for the relevant seasons; `PHI` is the separate 76ers franchise. **Wilt 1968** follows July 9 and all three players in the Philadelphia guide; [NBA's calendar explicitly adds undisclosed cash](https://www.nba.com/news/history-this-date-in-nba-july). No cash amount is guessed. [NBA’s Shaffer account](https://www.nba.com/news/kia-rookie-ladder-november-20-2024-edition) clarifies that his player rights were included after he left the league. The movement note and asset description state this explicitly; these are not draft rights, and no later Warriors appearances are asserted.
- **Melo 2011 is the final February 22 three-team deal**, not the February 21 preliminary outline. [Denver's completed release](https://www.nba.com/nuggets/news/nuggets_trade_02_22_2011.html), [Denver's guide](https://cdn.nba.com/teams/uploads/sites/1610612743/2024/01/DN_2324_MediaGuide.pdf), [Minnesota reporting](https://www.startribune.com/deal-s-done-koufos-added-to-trade-that-sends-brewer-to-nyc/116703294), and [the Koufos/second addition](https://www.nbcsports.com/nba/news/minor-addition-to-e2-80-98melo-trade-wolves-send-kosta-koufos-to-denver) establish all 13 players, both cash directions and the Denver 2015 second. [Denver's recap](https://www.nba.com/nuggets/news/carmelo_adventure_02_23_2011.html) identifies the two Golden State seconds and Denver cash; [Denver's roster-origin account](https://www.nba.com/nuggets/news/tracing-roots-behind-the-2018-19-nuggets-roster-032019) verifies the 2016 swap/Murray connection. The New York 2014 first is not falsely identified as Denver's Doug McDermott selection.
- **Bargnani 2013 is July 10** and includes Richardson's sign-and-trade, three players and three pick obligations: [completed report](https://www.hoopsrumors.com/2013/07/knicks-acquire-andrea-bargnani-1.html), [player ledger](https://www.shamsports.com/players/steve-novak), [second-round origins](https://sports.yahoo.com/blogs/nba-ball-dont-lie/york-knicks-trading-andrea-bargnani-because-course-155517381.html), [NBA's 2014 order](https://pr.nba.com/nba-draft-2014-order-of-selection/). [The pick ledger](https://www.basketballinsiders.com/nba-draft-picks-owed/) establishes the less-favorable NYK/DEN first. This is one composite entitlement, not two picks.
- **2016 swap allocation uses May 17 as the date the lottery fixed the positions**, not an asserted separately known administrative exercise date. [Contemporaneous result](https://www.espn.com/nba/draft2016/story/_/id/15581021/philadelphia-76ers-win-lottery-pick-first-2016-nba-draft) has NYK No. 7 to DEN and DEN No. 9 to TOR; [dated order](https://www.si.com/nba/2016/05/17/nba-draft-lottery-order-picks-results) confirms timing. Both entitlement-to-actual-pick conversions are recorded together, then both June 23 selections occur separately. The graph must not imply the swap creates an extra first.
- **Melo 2017/18** use the [Thunder announcement](https://www.nba.com/thunder/news/carmelo-anthony-170925), [Knicks guide](https://ak-static-int.nba.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2017/10/2017-18-Media-Guide-Knicks.pdf), [NBA completion notice](https://www.nba.com/news/carmelo-anthony-trade-hawks-dennis-schroeder-trade-thunder-official), [Philadelphia's announcement](https://www.nba.com/sixers/team-acquires-mike-muscala-three-team-deal) and [Atlanta guide](https://library.sfo2.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/publications/basketball/yearbooks/KATLHMG-2019.pdf). [NBA's Robinson account](https://www.nba.com/news/2018-summer-standouts-day-10-july-12) explicitly links the 2018 No. 36 to Anthony's 2017 deal. [The OKC pick ledger report](https://thunderousintentions.com/2021/06/19/okc-thunder-draft-picks-celtics-trade/) supplies the 2022 lottery protection and 2024/25 second-round fallback. Later resolution is not traced, and Anthony's Houston free agency is not represented as an Atlanta trade return.
